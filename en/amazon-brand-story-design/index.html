FAQ

Questions about this service

Does Brand Story replace A+ Content?

No. Amazon presents it as a complement: Brand Story focuses on brand and catalog while A+ explores the product.

Can it link to other products?

Depending on available features, it can highlight product lines and connect to product pages or the Store.

Do I need a long founder story?

No. Specific and verifiable information matters more than length.

Can you write the story for me?

We can structure copy from real information. We do not invent dates, certifications or claims.
